The generator scripts beside the fixtures document the prompts and transformations. The files
contain derived activations, logits, and token IDs—not checkpoint weight tensors. The pinned
[model card](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-12B-it-qat-q4_0-unquantized/blob/b6ed86275a6a5735884e208bfed95b445a684ca2/README.md)
identifies Google DeepMind as the author and declares the checkpoint
[Apache-2.0](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/apache_2).

For clarity, these four `.safetensors` fixtures are distributed under Apache-2.0 only, not the
repository's MIT option. Their directory-level license notice is in
[`native/hyperion_mlx/tests/fixtures/README.md`](native/hyperion_mlx/tests/fixtures/README.md).
Hyperion is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

## Other generated fixtures and dependencies

The tiny model under `crates/hyperion-model/fixtures/` and the tracked M1 benchmark corpus are
deterministically generated by project scripts; neither contains model checkpoint weights.
Third-party Rust, C++, Python, and build dependencies retain their own licenses. A packaged
binary distribution requires a separate dependency and notice audit.
